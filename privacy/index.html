Privacy policy

Effective September 17, 2026.

What the app stores

Everything you log — nights, runs, ratings, rankings, tags and notes, passport stamps, and the profile picture, banner and colour you choose — is stored on your phone, in the app's own storage. Nothing you log is sent to us or to anyone else, and there is no sync: what is on this phone stays on this phone. The one thing the app uploads is what you write on the feedback board, described below, and it is never anything you have logged.

Signing in

You can use the app without an account. If you create an account, it is through Sign in with Apple. Apple gives the app a stable identifier for you and, if you allow it, your name; the app keeps those on your phone to know whose data is whose. The app does not receive your email address unless you choose to share it, and it does not use it for anything. Creating an account in this version does not move your data off your phone; it gives your data an owner so that a future version can offer to keep it across devices, and that will be a separate choice with its own notice. Signing in does create an account on the feedback board's server, so that a post of yours can carry your name and so the board knows who its moderator is; that account holds your Apple identifier and the name the app shows for you, and nothing else.

The feedback board

Settings has a Feedback screen: a shared board where anyone using Frightfolio can suggest something, report a problem, reply to someone else's post and upvote. It is the only part of the app that uploads anything. Everything you log stays on your phone whether you ever use the board or not.

When you post, reply or upvote, this is what is sent and stored:

  • What you typed — a post's title and its words, or a reply's words. Everyone using the app can read them, so please do not put anything private in a post.
  • Your name, only if you are signed in. A post or reply made while you are signed in with Apple carries your account's identifier and the name the app shows for you. Made without an account, it carries neither, and shows as Anonymous. There is no third case.
  • A code for your copy of the app. The app sends a random identifier created on your phone the first time you use the board, and the server stores only a one-way scrambled version of it, never the identifier itself. It exists to do three things: keep one upvote to one phone, let the phone that wrote a post correct it for fifteen minutes, and stop the board being flooded. It is never shown to anyone, it is not an advertising identifier, it says nothing about you or your phone, and it cannot be turned back into anything. Deleting the app removes it.
  • When each of those happened.

Nothing else. Not your email address, not your location, not your phone's model, and nothing you have logged — no nights, runs, ratings, rankings, tags, notes or stamps ever go to the board.

The board is hosted for the app by Supabase, which stores this data on the app's behalf; like any web request, reaching it carries your phone's network address.

Posts and replies are moderated by the app's developer, who can change a post's status or remove a post or a reply from the board. Removing hides something from everyone; it is kept rather than erased, so a mistake can be undone. To have something you wrote taken off the board, or to ask what the board holds about you, write to the address at the end of this page and say which post it was.

If the app has no board set up, or cannot reach it, the Feedback screen says so and nothing is sent.

Wait times

While a Halloween Horror Nights season is running, the app fetches the current queue times for Universal Studios Florida from Queue-Times.com so that each house can show its wait. That request goes to Queue-Times.com's servers and, like any web request, carries your phone's network address; it carries nothing about you or what you have logged. Together with the feedback board, it is the only network request the app makes.

Analytics and crashes

The app includes no analytics or advertising software. Apple may collect app analytics and crash reports on the app's behalf only if you have opted in to sharing analytics with developers in your iPhone's settings, under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. That data reaches us through Apple, in aggregate, with nothing that identifies you.

What the app does not do

It does not track you across other apps or websites. It does not sell or share data. It does not use your location. It does not read your contacts, photos or calendar; if you choose a profile picture, only the picture you pick is stored.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your phone deletes everything it stored, including the code the feedback board used for this copy of the app. What you have already posted on the board stays on the board, because other people are reading it; write to the address below to have a post of yours taken down. Signing out keeps your data on the phone but blocks access to it until the same account signs in again, and signs you out of the board as well. To remove an account's association with Apple, use Sign in with Apple settings on your Apple Account page.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Universal Destinations & Experiences or NBCUniversal. House names and seasons appear as facts about the event; all artwork in the app is the app's own, and house descriptions are the app's own words with sources listed inside the app.
