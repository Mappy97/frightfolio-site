Privacy policy

Effective September 16, 2026.

What the app stores

Everything you log — nights, runs, ratings, rankings, tags and notes, passport stamps, and the profile picture, banner and colour you choose — is stored on your phone, in the app's own storage. Nothing you log is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no server behind the app in this version.

Signing in

You can use the app without an account. If you create an account, it is through Sign in with Apple. Apple gives the app a stable identifier for you and, if you allow it, your name; the app keeps those on your phone to know whose data is whose. The app does not receive your email address unless you choose to share it, and it does not use it for anything. Creating an account in this version does not move your data off your phone; it gives your data an owner so that a future version can offer to keep it across devices, and that will be a separate choice with its own notice.

Wait times

While a Halloween Horror Nights season is running, the app fetches the current queue times for Universal Studios Florida from Queue-Times.com so that each house can show its wait. That request goes to Queue-Times.com's servers and, like any web request, carries your phone's network address; it carries nothing about you or what you have logged. It is the only network request the app makes.

Analytics and crashes

The app includes no analytics or advertising software. Apple may collect app analytics and crash reports on the app's behalf only if you have opted in to sharing analytics with developers in your iPhone's settings, under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. That data reaches us through Apple, in aggregate, with nothing that identifies you.

What the app does not do

It does not track you across other apps or websites. It does not sell or share data. It does not use your location. It does not read your contacts, photos or calendar; if you choose a profile picture, only the picture you pick is stored.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your phone deletes everything it stored. Signing out keeps your data on the phone but blocks access to it until the same account signs in again. To remove an account's association with Apple, use Sign in with Apple settings on your Apple Account page.

Halloween Horror Nights

Frightfolio is an independent fan project for tracking your own nights at Halloween Horror Nights. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Universal Destinations & Experiences or NBCUniversal. House names and seasons appear as facts about the event; all artwork in the app is the app's own, and house descriptions are the app's own words with sources listed inside the app.
