Community rules

Reviews, posts and comments in Frightfolio are public: everyone using the app can read them. Profile pictures, banner photos and the line about you on public accounts are shown to people who are signed in. Before your first one, the app asks you to agree to these rules. They are the same rules the app shows, word for word.

The show is the point

Gore, monsters and scares from the houses are welcome. Real violence and real injuries are not.

Mark spoilers

Turn on Contains spoilers for anything that gives a house away. If you don't, Matt will.

No leaks

Nothing leaked, backstage or not meant to be public yet.

Be decent

No harassment, hate, threats or attacks on other people.

Keep it about the nights

No spam, ads or links to sell things.

Keep profiles clean

No nudity, hate symbols or real injuries in a profile picture or banner, or in the line about you.

13 and over

You need to be 13 or older to post.

How the rules are kept

Anyone can report a post or a profile. Matt reads reports every day, removes posts and pictures that break these rules and can remove accounts that keep breaking them. His decision is final.

When something of yours is removed, your Activity in the app says so and gives the reason. The person who reported it is never named. What is removed is kept for 90 days in case it needs another look, then deleted.

Contact

To reach Matt about a post or an account, email matthew.tucker.d+frightfolio@gmail.com. In the app, Settings → Community rules has the same address.
